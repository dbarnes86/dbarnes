What We're Building

The Vision

Refuge is not a holiday property. It is a private sanctuary — a place to step away from the noise, be part of a community that values craft and land, and build something that will outlast all of us.

I

Protected Sanctuary

A walled estate where families live on their own terms. Privacy. Peace. Real distance from the world. Designed for presence, not distraction.

II

Working Vineyard

1.5 hectares of AOC Bordeaux Merlot producing estate wine exclusively for members. Kitchen gardens, orchards, and real food from real soil in the heart of Saint-Émilion country.

III

Architect Lodges

Twelve individually designed eco-lodges across the estate. Natural materials, modern interiors. Solar. Water collection. Beautiful and built to endure for generations.

IV

Founding Community

Fifty families who value craft, independence, and quality. Winemaking, woodworking, ceramics, seasonal gatherings. A network forged by shared values, not circumstance.
